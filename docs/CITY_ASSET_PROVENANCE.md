# Focus City asset provenance

The FBX files under `artifacts/focusarx/public/city-assets/` were sourced from
`Sol4rOnGit/PocketCity` for the Focus City implementation.

The FocusArx product owner represented that they hold the required licence and
authorisation to use these files in FocusArx. This repository does not infer or
grant any additional rights in the source project. Keep this record with the
assets, and update it if the governing licence or attribution requirements are
provided separately.

Imported groups:

- residential and apartment buildings
- commercial shop and office buildings
- industrial factory and warehouse buildings
- straight road mesh
- power plant and water tower
- fire station, hospital, and police station

The original Unity C# runtime was not embedded in the browser application.
FocusArx uses a server-authoritative TypeScript simulation so progress, coin
spending, taxation, utilities, incidents, and focus-session advancement persist
through the existing account and PostgreSQL systems.
