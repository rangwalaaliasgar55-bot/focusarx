# FOCUSARX V2 - OBSESSION ENGINE

The current update is good, but we are still building a productivity app.

I want FocusArx to feel like a game, a personal journey, and a digital world.

Do NOT add random features.

Focus on retention, delight, progression, and emotional attachment.

---

## 1. EVOLVE FOCUS CITY INTO A LIVING WORLD

Current city is static.

Upgrade it.

### Requirements

* Day/night cycle
* Weather system
* Seasonal themes
* Animated buildings
* Citizens/NPCs
* Unlockable districts

Examples:

Level 1-10:

* Small town

Level 10-25:

* Study village

Level 25-50:

* Academic city

Level 50-100:

* Knowledge kingdom

Level 100+:

* Focus civilization

Every study session should visibly improve the world.

Users should WANT to come back to see it grow.

---
